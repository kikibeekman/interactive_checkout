Cleaner travel for your event *

Add a FlyBit to your ticket. Many of the artists, teams, speakers and visitors at Summit Stadium arrive by plane. Every FlyBit (€1) pays for 1 liter of non-fossil aviation fuel for Lowland Air’s flights, with around 80% less life-cycle CO₂ than fossil jet fuel. Lowland Air matches every FlyBit, so your €1 means 2 liters of non-fossil aviation fuel. Every FlyBit counts.
